CONTEXT & OWNERSHIP

The problem and my role.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Why the work mattered

A large-scale field rework effort had to align engineering drawing readiness, purchased components, internally manufactured materials and distribution across multiple global depots. Fragmented supply updates made it difficult to assess deployment readiness.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

What I owned

I owned the materials planning workstream, maintained shortage priorities and supply visibility, coordinated dependencies with engineering and manufacturing, and created an Oracle-connected Excel planning view for stakeholders.

SOLUTION DESIGN

How I approached it.

An end-to-end look at the planning logic, stakeholder workflow and delivery approach.

01

Define material readiness

Translated the rework scope into required components, shortage views and a changing supply plan.

02

Coordinate engineering dependencies

Worked with engineering on drawing releases that affected material procurement and internally manufactured part readiness.

03

Manage make and buy constraints

Tracked purchased-component schedules and in-house manufactured material requirements together.

04

Make depot allocations visible

Built an Excel tool with an Oracle data connection to help teams assess incoming material and planned deployment across field locations.

BUSINESS OUTCOMES

What changed.

Qualitative results are described without publishing unapproved internal performance figures.

  • Improved visibility into material timing and allocation dependencies for global depot decisions.
  • Supported coordinated execution of a complex rework initiative using regularly refreshed supply information.
